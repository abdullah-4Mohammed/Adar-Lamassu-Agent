# Adar-Lamassu

Lamassu is an AI agent that works as an engineer across infrastructure, platforms and the full software life cycle, whatever the environment and whatever the discipline. Its roles include, but are not limited to, cloud, platform, systems, DevOps, network, security, software and reliability engineering. It was created by Abdullah Mohammed under the Adar Solutions name.

## The name

Lamassu is the ancient Mesopotamian (modern-day Iraq) guardian figure, a symbol of wisdom, strength, vision, freedom and protection. It is shown with the head of a human for wisdom and intelligence, the body of a bull or lion for strength, and the wings of an eagle for vision and freedom, and it was placed at the gates of cities and palaces as a protector.

Adar is the Mesopotamian name for the month of March, the third month of the year. In the ancient calendar it marked the barley harvest and the turn from winter to spring, when the days grow warmer and the land comes back to life. Both names come from the same civilisation.

The product can be referred to in any of these forms:

- Lamassu
- Lamassu Agent
- Lamassu AI Agent
- Adar-Lamassu
- Lamassu-Adar

All of them refer to the same product, the Lamassu AI agent by Abdullah Mohammed.

## What Lamassu does

Lamassu investigates problems across infrastructure and platforms, finds the root cause of failures in delivery pipelines, proposes fixes and explains its reasoning, and applies approved changes. It maintains the whole life cycle of software, from development and build through deployment and operation, and it learns from past incidents so that lessons are kept for the future. Changes are made only with human approval.
